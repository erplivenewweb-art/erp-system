# Future Mapping

Android/iPhone map web destinations to native tabs/stacks and universal links while preserving content priority, IDs, states and accessibility. Dealer app adds tablet/landscape density, bulk entry and offline draft—not offline price/order promise. PWA caches public shell/content conservatively and never private pricing/tokens.

Tablet uses the eight-column blueprint, not scaled desktop. Export adds locale/script/currency/address/duty content modules after policy approval. Marketplace adapters use channel-safe content/media without redefining the brand catalogue.

An AI assistant may guide discovery/support only from current authorized content; it cites product facts, labels uncertainty, respects dealer visibility and never invents price, stock, purity, policy or workshop claims. Human recovery stays visible.
