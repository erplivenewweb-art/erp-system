# Guest to Loyal Customer Journey

| Stage | Intent | Friction to prevent | UX response | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Understand brand | Generic luxury claim | Manufacturer promise + evidence | Collection exploration |
| Search/category | Find relevant form | Taxonomy uncertainty | Suggestions, editorial categories | Relevant result click |
| Product | Judge suitability | Missing facts/trust | Media, safe facts, care/policy | Wishlist/cart intent |
| Wishlist/cart | Preserve decision | Forced registration | Guest persistence, clear refresh | Return or checkout |
| Checkout/payment | Complete safely | Surprise/duplicate action | Progressive review, idempotent language | One confirmation |
| Tracking | Reduce uncertainty | Opaque carrier state | Human timeline/support | Self-service success |
| Review | Share evidence | Burdensome form | Eligible concise prompt | Useful moderated review |
| Repeat | Re-engage | Manipulative personalization | Care/reorder/new discovery | Qualified return |
| Loyalty/referral | Earn/share future value | Hidden terms | Future explicit rules/consent | Voluntary use |
