# Photography Direction

Product: consistent neutral 4:5, accurate color/scale, complete angle set. Macro: texture, finishing and approved hallmarks without misleading magnification. Lifestyle: natural cultural context, restrained styling, inclusive hands/ages and no stereotype. Workshop/manufacturing: real tools/process with safety, consent and secret/privacy review. Packaging: actual included contents.

Hero/collection images use intentional negative space and separate mobile art direction. Dealer imagery emphasizes range, reliability and working partnership rather than consumer glamour. Video/reels use slow material detail, captions/transcript, poster frames and no essential spoken-only claim.

Lighting is soft directional with controlled highlights revealing silver form; backgrounds are warm ivory, charcoal or contextual natural material. Avoid blue-grey silver casts, excessive reflection cleanup, fake gemstone/material enhancement, composites implying contents, and AI imagery presented as workshop evidence.
