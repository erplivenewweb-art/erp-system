# Dealer Experience

Registration uses five progressive steps with save/resume, clear evidence purpose and an honest approval timeline. Dashboard prioritizes action: expiring quotations, pending decisions, active orders, dispatch and support—not vanity metrics.

Bulk search supports SKU alias/product text, keyboard entry, filters, list density and future upload preview. Catalogue shows authorized unit prices, MOQ, quantity slabs, freshness and bulk-cart state. Negotiation compares immutable revisions and highlights changes; accept/reject requires authority, current version and explicit terms.

Credit terms are view-only entitlements, never ledger balances. Bulk checkout is a staged review of address, delivery, price/tax/credit terms and authorized actor. Repeat orders copy intent then revalidate. Reports/analytics expose commerce-safe aggregates and accessible tables. Dealer support retains quotation/order context without exposing ERP internals.
