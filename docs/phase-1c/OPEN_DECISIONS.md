# Open Decisions Requiring Manual Approval

1. Brand/storefront to ERP company mapping and future multi-brand topology.
2. Initial online fulfilment branch and later allocation authority.
3. Retail and wholesale price ownership and approval workflow.
4. Silver-rate source, units, lock time, outage fallback, rounding and audit.
5. Making-charge rules and interaction with tax/GST.
6. Public availability wording, LOW_STOCK policy and stale-data thresholds.
7. Dealer evidence, GST/business verification provider, reviewers, retention and appeals.
8. Credit authority, limits, holds, partial payment and overdue behavior.
9. Quotation approval thresholds and conversion semantics.
10. Return, cancellation and refund policy and ERP/provider authority.
11. Customer/dealer/account/event/search/media retention and erasure schedule.
12. Object storage, CDN, media moderation and document vault provider.
13. Payment provider, supported methods, PCI boundary, webhook retention and COD policy.
14. Shipping/transport providers, branch disclosure policy, RTO and bulk logistics.
15. Export countries, currency/rate authority, duties, Incoterms, HS classification and compliance.
16. Marketplace identity, catalogue mapping, settlement and cancellation policy.
17. Analytics/consent providers, pseudonymization and AI usage policy.
18. Database technology/topology, migration tool, backup RPO/RTO and disaster recovery.

No legal, accounting, financial, pricing or retention rule is inferred by this design.
