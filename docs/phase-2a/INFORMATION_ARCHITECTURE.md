# Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

- Home
- Collections
  - Silver Sankha
  - Silver Pola
  - New and signature collections
- Products
  - All products
  - Categories
  - Gifting
- Custom Orders
- Manufacturing
  - Our workshop
  - Craft process
  - Materials and purity promise
  - Artisans
- Journal
- About
- Support
  - FAQ
  - Contact / approved WhatsApp
  - Order tracking
  - Care guide
  - Delivery and returns
- Wholesale
  - Dealer proposition
  - Registration
  - Dealer sign-in/portal

Utilities: search, customer account, wishlist and cart. Dealer portal is visually distinct and never mixed with customer account state.

## Mobile navigation

Bottom navigation contains Home, Discover, Search, Wishlist and Account/Cart decision pending research; cart remains persistently discoverable in header. The menu drawer holds Manufacturing, Custom Orders, Journal, About, Support, Policies and Wholesale. No destination exists only in hover/mega menu.

## Footer

Shop/collections; craft/about; service/support; wholesale; policies; contact/social; newsletter/consent; locale/currency if approved. Footer headings collapse accessibly on mobile but remain crawlable.

## Content rules

Every product has exactly one canonical collection context for breadcrumbs while allowing multiple discovery links. Search and facets never create uncontrolled indexable hierarchies. Policies have stable URLs and version/effective dates. Customer/dealer/private routes are excluded from public navigation/crawling.
