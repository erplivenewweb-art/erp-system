# CMS and Content Ownership

| Content | Editable by | Workflow / guard |
|---|---|---|
| Logo/brand marks | Brand owner | Versioned assets; legal approval |
| Hero/banners/festival themes | Campaign editor | Schedule, locale, contrast preview |
| Homepage sections | Brand editor | Curated modules; order/visibility |
| Product titles/descriptions/stories | Merchandiser | ERP facts read-only and separate |
| Collections/categories | Merchandiser | Slug/redirect and SEO review |
| Images/videos | Media editor | Rights, consent, crop, alt, captions |
| Blog/guides | Editorial | Draft-review-publish-version |
| Policies | Authorized policy owner | Version/effective date; legal review |
| FAQs/announcements | Support/content | Expiry and channel scope |
| Campaigns/coupons | Marketing + approval | No unapproved financial claims |
| Dealer/wholesale pages | Dealer content owner | Public vs approved visibility preview |
| Reviews | Moderator | Evidence, reason and audit |

CMS preview must show mobile/tablet/desktop, locale, public/customer/dealer visibility, scheduled state and contrast. Editors cannot change ERP weight, purity, size, stock, billing, GST or internal identifiers. All publication is versioned, optimistic and audited; unpublish preserves links/redirect decisions.
