# Future-Ready Experience

The same content model, tokens, components and journeys adapt by capability—not device forks.

- Android/iPhone: mobile navigation, touch targets, PKCE identity, deep links, safe push, offline read cache and native share reuse channel-neutral concepts.
- Dealer app/tablet: denser catalogue/table modes, barcode-free public IDs, large quantity entry, quotation comparison and landscape layouts.
- PWA: install/offline is progressive; never cache private prices, tokens or stale transactional promises.
- Export: locale, script, currency, units, address, tax/duty and delivery language expand without changing hierarchy. Cultural/legal claims are region-reviewed.
- Marketplace: core imagery/content has channel-safe renditions; marketplace constraints do not reshape the primary brand site.
- Future AI: conversational discovery cites current product/content facts, labels uncertainty, respects visibility/consent and never invents purity, stock, price or craft claims.
- New collections: taxonomy, story modules, media ratios and product facts are composable rather than Silver Sankha-specific templates.

Future capabilities remain optional and require separate research, security, accessibility and policy approval.
