# Photography Direction

Product: accurate neutral 4:5 masters, front/three-quarter/side/macro/scale/context/packaging. Hero: narrative wide 16:9 plus art-directed 4:5 mobile. Lifestyle: culturally grounded, natural gesture, inclusive ages/skin, no stereotype. Workshop/artisan/manufacturing: real environment, consent, safe process and secret/privacy review. Dealer: breadth, consistency and partnership rather than consumer glamour.

Lighting is large soft directional with controlled edge highlights; silver remains neutral, not blue. Backgrounds: warm ivory, charcoal or honest contextual material. Props are sparse and culturally accurate. Camera angles preserve scale; macro captions contextualize magnification. Depth of field keeps decision-relevant product detail sharp.

Color grade is warm-neutral, consistent white point and realistic material. Do not remove meaningful texture, fabricate marks, composite included items or use AI imagery as manufacturing evidence. Video/360 has stable exposure, captions/transcript, static fallback and poster. Social/reels derive from the same truth/rights system.
