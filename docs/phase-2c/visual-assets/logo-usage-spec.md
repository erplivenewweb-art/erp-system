# Logo Usage Asset Specification

Design-only construction sheet requirements:

- Primary horizontal, stacked, symbol and monochrome lockups.
- Master vector source plus print-safe PDF and raster exports generated later by an approved designer.
- Clear-space unit: one symbol-height/x-height around all sides.
- Digital minimum: provisional 120px wordmark and 24px symbol, subject to legibility research.
- Approved surfaces: Ivory/white with Ink mark; Ink/charcoal with white mark; photographic placement only on controlled quiet field.
- Prohibited: stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, gradients, unapproved festival recolor, low contrast, wordmark recreation.
- Every export records asset ID, version, owner, rights, color mode and retirement status.

No logo artwork is invented in Phase 2C; name/trademark and final form require approval.
