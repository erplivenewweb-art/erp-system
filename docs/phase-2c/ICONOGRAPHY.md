# Iconography

Outline icons are default: 1.5px optical stroke, round joins where appropriate, 16/20/24/32px boxes and consistent baseline. Filled icons are reserved for selected/toggled states and small semantic emphasis, never a second unrelated family.

Commerce set: search, account, wishlist, cart, delivery, return, care, zoom, video. Dealer: organization, catalogue, MOQ/quantity, quotation, revision, credit terms, dispatch, analytics. Manufacturing: tool, workshop, quality check, material, craft step. Trust: verified purchase, documented purity promise, secure service, packaging—only with evidence.

Icons have 8px minimum text spacing and 44px interactive container. Ambiguous icons require visible labels. Decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology; meaningful icons have accessible names through the control. Motion is ≤160ms state morph/opacity, never continuous, and removed under reduced motion.
