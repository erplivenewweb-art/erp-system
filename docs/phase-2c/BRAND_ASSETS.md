# Luxury Brand Assets

Logo system requires primary horizontal, stacked, monochrome and symbol marks after trademark/design approval. Clear space equals one symbol-height/x-height unit; provisional minimums: wordmark 120px digital, symbol 24px, print dimensions to be tested. Never stretch, outline, shadow, recolor outside approved tokens or place over uncontrolled detail.

Favicons require 16/32/48 plus scalable source; app icons use platform safe zones and no tiny wordmark. Social covers have channel crop-safe masters. Email branding uses lightweight hosted mark, system-safe type and accessible text. Packaging uses print-qualified vector/master, material/finish proofs and legal marks.

Invoice branding is ERP-owned and **not changed by this phase**; only a future approved brand usage recommendation may be provided to ERP owners. Dealer branding is co-branding-safe, clearly distinguishes approved partnership and forbids logo downloads without rights. Asset register tracks version, owner, rights, usage, locale and retirement.
