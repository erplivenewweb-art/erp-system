# Typography

Display serif conveys editorial craft; sans serif carries body, commerce, forms, navigation, tables, dealer portal and CMS. Recommended families remain subject to licensing and Indian-script review: Cormorant Garamond/Noto Serif Bengali/Georgia; Inter/Noto Sans Bengali/system UI.

Scale: display 76/84, 64/72, 56/64; H1 48/56; H2 40/48; H3 32/40; H4 24/32; H5 20/28; H6 18/26; body 18/28, 16/25, 14/22; caption/overline 12 with accessible use. Mobile uses responsive scaling without dropping body below 16px.

Headings use −1% to −2% tracking; overlines +14% and very short; body normal. Price/metrics use tabular numerals. Maximum editorial measure 720px/45–75 characters. Dealer tables keep 14px minimum and offer responsive cards.

Self-host WOFF2, preload one critical body face at most, swap with metric-compatible fallbacks, subset by approved locale without removing required shaping glyphs. Font failure must remain readable and layout-stable.
