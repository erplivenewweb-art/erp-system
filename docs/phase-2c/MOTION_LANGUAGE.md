# Motion Language

Motion expresses causality and craft pace. Page change is immediate with optional 160–240ms fade; hero uses one ≤420ms entrance; scroll reveal is subtle and never required. Hover moves 2px/underline; press 80ms/2px. Wishlist/cart states morph ≤160ms with textual confirmation. Gallery crossfade/slide follows direction and preserves control.

Navigation/dropdowns/drawers open ≤240ms and transfer focus after visible. Form feedback appears in-flow, no shake. Checkout/dealer transitions emphasize current step without celebration overload. Loading is stable skeleton/pulse; success is restrained; error is static and actionable.

Curves: standard cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), enter (0,0,.2,1), exit (.4,0,1,1), editorial out (.16,1,.3,1). No scroll hijack, autoplay sound, infinite primary-view loops or dedicated heavy animation dependency. Reduced motion removes translate, auto-advance and shimmer. INP target remains ≤200ms.
