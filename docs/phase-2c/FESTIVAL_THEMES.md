# Festival Theme Engine

Themes are controlled accent/content packs, never layout forks.

- Diwali: ember/russet accent, warm points of light, no flashing.
- Durga Puja: deep vermilion, respectful textile/pandal abstraction after cultural review.
- Raja Festival: berry accent and regional editorial content after terminology review.
- Akshaya Tritiya: antique-gold accent with verified relevance, no investment claims.
- Wedding Season: deep garnet, gifting/ritual content without stereotype.
- Christmas: forest accent and gifting imagery appropriate to audience.
- New Year: midnight blue, renewal/story emphasis.

Each pack declares token overrides, hero/media set, texture, locale/audience, start/end, owner, approvals and fallback. Semantic/focus colors, grid, type hierarchy, product accuracy and accessibility cannot be overridden. Preview, performance budget and automatic expiry are mandatory.
