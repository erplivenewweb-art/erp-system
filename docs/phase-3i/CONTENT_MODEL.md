# Content Model

Central synthetic fixtures define product drafts, collection drafts, categories, reserved media and blog drafts. Managers provide add/edit/delete, draft/publish, feature, visibility and sort placeholders without state or persistence.

Homepage content covers hero heading/description/CTAs, trust, manufacturing, packaging and testimonial placeholders. Additional managers own banners, header/footer/dealer navigation, footer contact/social/policy/newsletter fields and homepage section ordering.

No real product, media, customer, SEO record or blog content is represented.
