# Footer

The footer contains approved shell-level placeholders for the brand/manufacturing story, Discover, Our Making, Customer Care, Wholesale, Policies, Contact and social channels. Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest are internal placeholder destinations until ownership and final URLs are approved.

Newsletter markup includes a persistent email label, email autocomplete, 44px input/button and explicit text that processing is not connected. No form handler, subscriber storage or network call exists.

The floating WhatsApp action is a 44px minimum target and remains a placeholder navigation link. Final phone ownership and approved deep link are content/provider decisions.
