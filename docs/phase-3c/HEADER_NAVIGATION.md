# Header and Navigation

Desktop header content: CMS-ready logo placeholder; Home, Collections, Shop, Craftsmanship, Manufacturing, Custom Orders and Wholesale; Search, Wishlist, Cart and Account utility links; distinct Wholesale action; About, Guides, Contact and future Blog/Reviews/Dealer destinations.

Collections and Shop use native `details`/`summary` disclosure-based mega menus with direct overview and child links. They remain operable by pointer and the browser's native disclosure keyboard model, expose content without hover-only access, and keep the open panel inside the desktop viewport. At laptop/tablet/mobile widths, desktop navigation collapses into a native modal drawer. The drawer provides every destination, nested discovery links and utility actions.

Drawer behavior: first-control focus on open, bidirectional Tab trap, native cancel/Escape handling, explicit close, trigger focus restoration, 44px controls, responsive width, backdrop and reduced-motion transition removal. Links are navigation-only and point to future routes; no page or business behavior is implemented.
